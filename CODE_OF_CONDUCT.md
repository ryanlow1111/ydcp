# Code of Conduct

YDCP follows the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)**.

In short: be kind and welcoming, respect different views and experience levels, give and accept feedback gracefully, and focus on what's best for the community. Harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, and sharing other people's private information are not accepted. This also applies to how models and characters are discussed and shown.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If you experience or see unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer privately through [GitHub](https://github.com/ryanlow1111). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, issues or contributions, and may block people who break this code, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
